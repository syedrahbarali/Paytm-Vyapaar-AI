## 🎯 Jury Q&A Defense Matrix (Top 4 Tough Questions & Ready Answers)

### Q1 (Jury): "Customer dukan par bolega udhaar de do, lekin QR scan karke pay-later confirm nahi kiya to?"
- **Answer (Salwa):** *"Dukandar samaan tab tak counter se nahi uthayega jab tak Soundbox bol na de: 'Sharma Kirana alert: Rahul Kumar ne ₹200 udhaar darj kiya!' Soundbox ka audio confirmation hi dukandar ke liye physical delivery ka token hai."*

### Q2 (Jury): "Customer Paytm app use nahi kar raha, PhonePe ya Google Pay se scan kar raha hai to?"
- **Answer (Rahbar):** *"Counter QR hamara Paytm Smart Web Checkout URL (`paytm.me/pay/store`) embed karta hai. Kisi bhi phone camera ya standard UPI scanner se scan karne par browser me mobile checkout khulta hai, jahan se customer Pay-Later select kar sakta hai. 100% device agnostic hai."*

### Q3 (Jury): "AI math me hallucinate karega to dukandar ka hisaab galat ho jayega?"
- **Answer (Rahbar):** *"Hum LLM se math karwate hi nahi hain. LLM sirf user ka intent classify karta hai ('SALES_QUERY' ya 'KHATA_QUERY'). Exact aggregation backend me SQLite/PostgreSQL deterministic SQL chalta hai: `SELECT SUM(amount)`. 0% hallucination guaranteed."*

### Q4 (Jury): "Agar customer next visit par settle karne se mana kar de to?"
- **Answer (Salwa):** *"Checkout screen par option uncheck ho sakta hai, lekin app me customer ka risk tag 'CRITICAL' mark ho jata hai aur dukandar ko 1-click WhatsApp payment reminder dispatch karne ka option milta hai jisme dynamic QR link hota hai."*
